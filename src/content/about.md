I'm Rining Wu, a PhD researcher working across AI and computational neuroscience, with affiliations to the University of Leeds and the University of Birmingham.

My doctoral research focuses on how neurons respond to changing visual scenes. I use machine learning to model neural responses, and have co-authored studies on protein solubility and sensor-based flow prediction.

## Research

- **Neural responses to natural video.** [Vi-ST](/publications/aligning-neuronal-coding-of-dynamic-visual-scenes-with-foundation-vision-models/) combines vision-model features with temporal processing to predict retinal neuronal responses. The paper was accepted at ECCV 2024. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-73223-2_14) · [Code](https://github.com/wurining/Vi-ST)
- **Protein solubility.** [HybridGCN](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-023-00788-8), published in the Journal of Cheminformatics in 2023, combines learned protein features with biophysical features.
- **Sensor time series.** Two 2024 papers examine machine learning for gas–water flow prediction: [IEEE Sensors Journal](https://doi.org/10.1109/JSEN.2024.3475292) and [Thermal and Fluids Engineering Conference](https://doi.org/10.1615/tfec2024.ml.050649).
